## PROCEDURE:

### STEP-1: Creating Base Flange and Bottom Relief

* Base profile sketch Start a sketch on the XY (Top) Plane and draw the outer flange boundary with an overall vertical length of 120 mm and a central waist width of 40 mm. Add the four corner mounting lobes with R12 outer fillets and the two horizontal ears spanning 90 mm overall with R10 ends.
* Flange extrusion Extrude the closed base profile upward by 15 mm.
* Bottom recess Start a sketch on the bottom face, offset or project the inner 110 mm relief boundary shown in Section A-A, and cut upward by 3 mm.

### STEP-2: Creating Central Hub and Side Bosses

* Central hub revolve Create a sketch on the XZ (Front) Plane for the central hub profile, extending to a total height of 85 mm from the bottom face. Include the Ø55 mm upper cylinder and the 120° angled conical transition meeting the base flange, then revolve 360° around the vertical center axis.
* Vertical mounting ears Sketch two R12 bosses spaced 100 mm apart along the vertical centerline on the top face of the 15 mm flange. Extrude them upward by 5 mm so their top faces sit 20 mm above the bottom face.
* Horizontal mounting ears Sketch two R10 bosses spaced 70 mm apart along the horizontal centerline on the base flange. Extrude them upward by 10 mm so their top faces sit 25 mm above the bottom face.

### STEP-3: Creating  Central Bores and Counterbores

* Top counterbore On the top face of the Ø55 mm hub, sketch a Ø40 mm circle and extrude-cut downward by 30 mm. Add the upper stepped rim detail (Ø45 mm inner step and Ø50 mm outer lip) shown in Section A-A.
* Bottom counterbore On the bottom face, sketch a Ø30 mm circle concentric with the hub and extrude-cut upward by 30 mm.
* Center through-hole Sketch a Ø20 mm circle at the center and cut completely through the remaining web to connect the top and bottom bores.

### STEP-4: Creating Mounting Holes

* Vertical ear holes Use the Hole tool on the two vertical ears (100 mm center-to-center) to create 2 × Ø12 mm through-holes with Ø20 mm counterbores at a depth of 15 mm.
* Horizontal ear holes Use the Hole tool on the two horizontal ears (70 mm center-to-center) to create 2 × Ø10 mm simple through-holes.
* Flange corner holes Use the Hole tool on the four flange corner lobes (Section C-C) to create 4 × Ø10 mm through-holes with Ø20 mm counterbores.
 
### STEP-5:Creating Stiffening Ribs and Fillets

* Stiffening ribs Create sketches on the XZ and YZ midplanes connecting the outer wall of the central hub to the top faces of the vertical and horizontal bosses. Use the Rib tool with a thickness of 5 mm directed downward into the body.
* Cast fillets Apply the Fillet tool with a radius of R2 along all intersecting edges between the hub, ribs, bosses, and base flange as indicated across Sections A-A, B-B, and C-C.
